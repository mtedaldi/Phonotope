Phonotope Spike 0.0: benchmark mode

Runs a ladder of audio loads, one after another, and measures how fast the audio clock falls behind the wall clock in each stage. Back to the background test.

Keep the page in the foreground with the screen on (a screen wake lock is requested where available). Use a low volume. You will hear a tone, noise, drops, and murmuring voices; listen for crackles and gaps and note in which stage they start.

Settings

Idle.

Results

Lag rate: how much slower than real time the audio clock runs (0 % is perfect). Big steps: seconds in which the audio clock lost more than 30 ms. Output timestamp rate is a second, independent estimate if the browser supports it.

Environment

Log

Spike only. The voices are crude load generators, not a design for the vocal texture generator.
