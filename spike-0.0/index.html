Phonotope Spike 0.0

Throwaway test: does Web Audio keep generating sound reliably with the screen off? Noise with slow modulation, plus randomly timed drops through a convolution reverb. The event scheduler uses a look-ahead against the audio clock.

Benchmark mode: an automated load ladder that shows what this device can sustain in real time.

Before you start: set a low volume and keep the phone unplugged for battery measurements. Do not use a wake lock; the screen must be allowed to turn off.
